As Southeast Asia solidifies its position as a frontier for AI innovation, Freyr
AI has announced a strategic partnership with Anshtern Singapore. This alliance
unites Freyr AI's proven AI infrastructure platform with Anshtern Singapore's
full-stack AI-native capabilities—including its flagship DreamCloud Embodied AI
Cloud Platform—to support broader AI adoption across the region. Together, the
two parties will deliver scalable, cost-effective compute tailored to the
evolving needs of enterprises and government-backed initiatives throughout
Southeast Asia.

## Expanding AI Compute Across Key Southeast Asian Markets

The partnership targets key growth markets, including Thailand, Malaysia, and
Indonesia, focusing on joint market development and the delivery of
scenario-based solutions. By pooling complementary resources, the partners will
refine standardized offerings such as GPU leasing and intelligent computing
hosting, anchored by Freyr AI's state-of-the-art data center in Thailand and
extended across its Southeast Asia compute footprint. These facilities are
engineered to support rack-scale GPU deployments, bare-metal hosting, and
high-density cluster operations essential for modern AI workloads.

## Bridging Virtual Training and Physical Deployment

Central to the collaboration is the integration of Freyr AI's NVIDIA Reference
Architecture-powered infrastructure with Anshtern Singapore's flagship
DreamCloud Embodied AI Cloud Platform. DreamCloud uniquely combines
physics-based simulation, cross-terminal robot orchestration, and distributed
computing scheduling to bridge the gap between virtual training and physical
deployment. This synergy addresses the acute shortage of high-fidelity
simulation resources in Southeast Asia, significantly reducing R&D cycles and
time-to-market for enterprises in robotics, industrial automation, and
autonomous driving.

## Building a Connected Regional AI Ecosystem

To foster a cohesive ecosystem, the alliance will establish regular business and
technical exchange mechanisms and jointly develop a cross-regional computing
power scheduling platform. Highlighting these efforts, the partners will host
the Global Computing Ecosystem Conference in Q4 2026 in Singapore, convening
industry leaders, policymakers, and investors to shape the future of AI data
centers (AIDC) and regional AI policy.

"Regional collaboration is the cornerstone of scaling AI effectively," said a
spokesperson for Freyr AI. "By joining forces with Anshtern Singapore, we are
building an integrated infrastructure foundation that empowers businesses
across Southeast Asia to innovate faster, more securely, and at scale."
