Ian Wong, CFA, CFO of Freyr AI, joined industry leaders at The Tech Capital's APAC Finance Forum 2026 in Singapore for a panel discussion on "The Neocloud Boom: Financing the Next Generation of AI Compute."

Moderated by Rachit Mohan, Managing Director, Data Centre Leasing, APAC at JLL, the session brought together perspectives from S&P Global, Antares Ventures and Freyr AI to examine how the rapid growth of neoclouds is reshaping the financing and valuation of AI infrastructure.

![Panel discussion at APAC Finance Forum 2026](./APAC-Finance-Forum-2026-group-photo-sit.jpg#full)

## Rethinking Investment Frameworks for AI Infrastructure

The discussion explored a central question for the next phase of AI infrastructure investment: can neocloud platforms deliver infrastructure grade returns?

As GPU platforms and AI factories continue to scale, traditional infrastructure investment frameworks are being tested by shorter technology cycles, utilisation risk, customer concentration and rapidly evolving compute economics. The panel discussed how financing models, risk assessment and capital structures may need to evolve alongside this new generation of digital infrastructure.

## Supporting Scalable AI Compute Across APAC

Freyr AI continues to engage with partners across the technology, infrastructure and financial ecosystem to support the sustainable development of scalable AI compute across APAC.
